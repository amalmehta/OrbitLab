Hohmann Transfer

Two burns move you between circular orbits for the least Δv: one to enter an ellipse that touches both orbits, one to circularize at the far side. Changing inclination too? The tilt is split between the burns, mostly at the far, slow one.

Gravity Assist

Swing past the Moon to change your orbit around Earth without burning fuel. The planner picks your parking orbit's node so the Moon is in its plane when you arrive, then searches for when to fire so you pass at the altitude and side you choose.

Rendezvous

Catch a space station: line up your orbit's plane with its plane if they differ, wait until it leads by just the right angle, transfer up, and match its velocity. You arrive 40 m behind the docking port.

Docking Agent
